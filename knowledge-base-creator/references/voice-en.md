# Voice rules: English

These rules are fixed. Apply them to the files you write, to your messages to the user, and copy them into `00-permanent-rules.md` and into the output constraints of `04-runtime-ai-context.md`, so the app copy and the runtime AI follow them too.

- Plain English, second person ("you"), no padding. Flowing sentences linked by commas, colons and semicolons, not a string of very short detached sentences.
- Active voice. Titles in sentence case, verbs in the present tense.
- Never use the em dash. No emoji, no hashtags, no decorative asterisks, no exclamation marks for enthusiasm.
- Banned words: leverage, landscape, ecosystem, robust, crucial, seamless, delve.
- No "not only X but also Y" constructions, no automatic lists of three, no superlatives or needless adjectives, no metaphors or stock phrases.
- Do not open sentences with "And".
- Interface labels say what happens: "Save today's assessment", not "Save". Errors explain the reason and what to do, without apologies.

Verify marker for this language: `[to verify]`.
